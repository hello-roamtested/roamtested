## Plans and pricing

As of 23 October 2026, <Aff provider="en/tripcom">Trip.com</Aff> lists more than ten China eSIM plans.

Inside each plan you choose two things: daily data or one total allowance, and the number of days. You can pick anywhere from 1 to 30 days, and some plans also offer 60, 90 or 365 days. With daily data you get a fresh allowance every 24 hours from activation; once it runs out, speed drops to 384 kbps until the next allowance. A total package suits trips where your use is uneven — light most days, heavy on a few.

I've bought the 500MB-a-day plan multiple times, in July, August and October 2026, and paid $0.10 a day each time.

Two details that are easy to get wrong:

- **Refunds:** Trip.com refunds an eSIM any time before you install it. Installing it, not activating it, ends the refund window — so if your plans might change, install it close to departure.
- **Staying longer:** you can buy a renewal for the same eSIM in the app. It starts automatically when the current plan ends, with no new QR code, though you may need to restart your phone. A renewal can't be moved to a different phone.
